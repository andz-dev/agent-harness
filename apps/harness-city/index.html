Harness City

Every repository is a city. Its seven districts are the seven harness pillars, built up from a vacant lot (L0) to a skyline (L3). Traffic shows known limits, construction sites show the next planned fix, and decay shows entropy findings.

score = Σ level ÷ 3 × weight − entropy penalty
